Conversion elastic bidding

+15%campaign spend

Static bids leave advertiser budget stranded on campaigns that could absorb more spend efficiently, and overspend where they can’t.

I led the 0-to-1 launch of conversion elastic bidding, adjusting bids dynamically to maximize cost-per-conversion efficiency, and measured the impact with difference-in-differences.

Result: +15% spend on campaigns.

How I knew it was real: A bidding change is hard to A/B cleanly, because treated and control campaigns compete in the same auction and treatment leaks across the boundary. Difference-in-differences was the identification strategy chosen to handle that, rather than a split test that would have quietly measured the wrong thing.
