Payment-page ads

$75Mrevenue, tripled

An ad placement on a partner’s payment page only ships if the partner believes it will not cost them checkout conversions. That belief, rather than advertiser demand, was the constraint on the placement’s revenue.

I productized do-no-harm holdout experiments that proved no impact on checkout-funnel conversion, cutting partner onboarding time by two-thirds.

Result: Payment-page ad revenue tripled to $75M.

How I knew it was real: This experiment’s job was to prove a null, not a lift — so it had to be powered to detect a harm small enough that the partner would care about it. A non-significant result from an underpowered test would have told them nothing, and they would have been right not to trust it.
