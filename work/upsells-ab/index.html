Upsells A/B program

+20%revenue per transaction, above client baseline

Rokt Upsells had to beat the client’s existing baseline before it could earn wider rollout, and the formats and offers driving it were levers nobody had tested systematically.

I designed the A/B experimentation program that accelerated hypothesis testing on formats and offers, running across roughly 2 million transactions a day.

Result: +20% revenue per transaction above client baseline.

How I knew it was real: The comparison is the client’s own baseline, not a synthetic control. At roughly 2 million transactions a day the binding constraint was never sample size — it was how fast hypotheses could be queued and read, which is what the program was built to fix.
